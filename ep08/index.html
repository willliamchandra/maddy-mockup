Concept

One road, two moods, one honest verdict

Nanjing Road is the city's most famous shopping street and the one people call a tourist trap. That question is the hook, and her "banyak yang bilang overrated, but…" voice is made for it. Walk it west to east: calm temple and luxury malls by day, then the pedestrian street's neon at night. End on a verdict, five minutes from the Bund where EP.07 finished.

Pace, sound, grade

  • Cuts: slower in the west (temple, malls, 1.5–2 s), faster on the pedestrian street (1 s). The road's mood shows in the edit.
  • Day to night: one match cut on the same storefront or sign if she has both. Otherwise a whip-pan.
  • Speed ramp: into the first bite in F6 and the neon reveal in F8.
  • Music: trending audio from the IG audio tab, about 15 dB under the VO. Drop it out for one beat right before the verdict.
  • Grade: neutral daytime, let the neon stay saturated at night. No heavy filter.
