Concept

Afternoon to night, ending on the Bund lights

Part I covered the daytime café and shopping strip (Wukang Rd → Anfu → Huaihai → Xintiandi). Part II picks up at Xintiandi and walks east into the evening, so the reel has a built-in payoff: the Bund skyline switching on. Open on that payoff, rewind, then earn it.

Use this

Cold-open the ending

Bund skyline at night for 2 s, VO: "Part 2 kita tutup di sini… tapi start-nya dari mana?" then a rewind effect to daytime. Shows the payoff before anyone can scroll away.

Pace, sound, grade

  • Cuts: 1–2 s per clip, cut on footsteps. Whip-pan between stops.
  • Rewind: CapCut "Rewind" or "VHS rewind" effect for F1 → F2, under 0.5 s.
  • Speed ramp: slow-mo into the xiaolongbao bite and the moment the Bund lights come on.
  • Music: pick a trending city-walk audio in the IG audio tab when posting. Keep it about 15 dB under the VO and bring it up to full on F8.
  • Grade: warm daytime, slightly cooler night. Keep skin tones natural. No heavy filter, her feed doesn't use one.
  • Export: 1080×1920, 30 fps. Keep text inside the middle 1080×1350 so the profile grid crop doesn't cut it.
